InvoiceManagementSystem
This project was created to strengthen my understanding of object-oriented programming in C#. The application manages customers, invoices, and invoice items while tracking total revenue through a centralized invoice management system.

System Design
Customer ↓ Invoice ↓ InvoiceItems

The Invoice class contains customer information and a collection of invoice items. The InvoiceManager is responsible for tracking invoices and calculating overall revenue.
